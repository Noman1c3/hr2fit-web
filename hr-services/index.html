Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About HR Outsourcing

How does HR2fit deliver services on site?

Unlike traditional outsourced HR providers who only respond via remote ticket queues, HR2fit consultants physically come to your business in Massachusetts or New Hampshire. We meet directly with you, coach your managers, and support your team on the ground.

Can we start with one service and expand later?

Yes, absolutely. Our layered service approach gives you total flexibility. You might start with recruiting or employee handbook creation today, and expand into full payroll administration, compensation benchmarking, or compliance coaching as your team grows.

How does HR2fit price its services?

Our services are priced to fit small and mid-sized business budgets, typically structured around your employee count and scope of support. There are no surprise fees or massive executive retainers.

What is included in the free on-site HR assessment?

A confidential, comprehensive evaluation conducted directly at your facility. We review your current employee handbooks, onboarding procedures, payroll classifications, and compliance exposure, delivering actionable recommendations with zero obligation.
