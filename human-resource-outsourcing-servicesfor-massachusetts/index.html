State Compliance Shield

Navigating Massachusetts Labor Laws Without In-House Overhead

Failure to comply with Commonwealth employment mandates can lead to mandatory triple damages, individual manager liability, and severe MCAD penalties.

Paid Family & Medical Leave (PFML)

Managing state contribution calculations, exemption filings, intermittent leave approvals, and job protection requirements under the Massachusetts Department of Family and Medical Leave.

Earned Sick Time Law

Ensuring accurate accrual tracking (1 hour for every 30 hours worked), distinguishing paid vs. unpaid sick leave based on headcount, and preventing unlawful retaliation claims.

Massachusetts Equal Pay Act (MEPA)

Conducting pay equity self-evaluations, eliminating salary history questions in hiring interviews, and documenting legitimate, gender-neutral wage differentials.

Noncompetition Agreement Act

Drafting enforceable non-disclosure and restrictive covenants that strictly adhere to Massachusetts garden leave rules, 10-day notice requirements, and legal counsel rights.

Strict 3-Prong Independent Contractor Test

Protecting your company from MA's aggressive independent contractor statute (M.G.L. c. 149, § 148B) to prevent catastrophic misclassification fines and back-wage liability.

MCAD Complaint Handling & Training

Handling workplace harassment complaints, conducting confidential internal investigations, and delivering mandatory annual anti-harassment training to staff.
