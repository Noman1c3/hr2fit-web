The Hiring Dilemma

The Real Cost of Hiring on Your Own vs. Partnering with HR2fit

Hiring is one of the highest-leverage activities in any company, but running a search without an HR team pulls leadership away from core revenue-generating work.

✕ The In-House Hiring Grind

  • 40 to 60 hours lost: Writing ads, posting across job boards, sorting 150+ unqualified resumes, and fielding candidate emails.
  • Candidate ghosting: High drop-off rates due to delayed response times and unstructured communication.
  • Expensive mis-hires: Hiring out of sheer fatigue or urgency, resulting in turnover that costs up to 1.5x the employee’s salary.
  • No formal background checks: Potential compliance liabilities with state/federal FCRA background check rules.
  • Chaotic onboarding: New hires arrive on day one with no clear roadmap, handbook, or training plan.

✓ The HR2fit Recruiting Advantage

  • Zero wasted executive hours: We build targeted job descriptions, launch multi-channel campaigns, and conduct all preliminary screening.
  • Curated top 3 candidates: You only spend time interviewing candidates who have already passed rigorous skill and culture screenings.
  • Industry-specific expertise: Deep networks in Legal, Dental, Skilled Trades, and Small Business across MA & NH.
  • Full compliance & vetting: Compliant reference checks, salary benchmarking, and offer letter structuring.
  • Integrated onboarding: Seamless hand-off with employee handbooks, job descriptions, and benefit enrollment ready on day one.
