Practical HR Wisdom

HR2fit's 10 Do's & Don'ts for Small Business HR Management

Essential guidelines every business owner should implement to safeguard their company, build team trust, and avoid costly regulatory penalties.

The 5 Critical "Do's"

  • Establish clear written job descriptions: Define exact responsibilities, FLSA classifications, and performance benchmarks for every role from day one.
  • Update your employee handbook annually: Keep policies synchronized with constantly evolving Massachusetts and New Hampshire state labor laws.
  • Conduct objective, documented reviews: Institute regular feedback cycles and engagement measures so employees always know where they stand.
  • Separate medical and personnel records: Strictly isolate confidential health information, I-9 documents, and disciplinary records in secure files.
  • Investigate complaints immediately: Treat all harassment, discrimination, or wage disputes with confidential, documented, and fair protocols.

The 5 Costly "Don'ts"

  • Don't misclassify workers as 1099 contractors: State departments of labor heavily penalize businesses that treat employees as independent contractors.
  • Don't copy generic online templates: Outdated internet handbook templates frequently violate local state-specific sick leave and non-compete laws.
  • Don't let toxic behavior linger: Avoiding difficult conversations with poor performers demoralizes your top talent and damages culture.
  • Don't conduct unauthorized background checks: Running credit or criminal history checks without strict FCRA disclosures creates severe legal liability.
  • Don't terminate without documentation: Firing an employee without a clear paper trail of prior performance notices dramatically raises wrongful termination risk.
