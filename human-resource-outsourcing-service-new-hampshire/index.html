Granite State Compliance

Key New Hampshire Employment Laws We Safeguard Your Business Against

NHDOL audits are rigorous and unannounced. We ensure your policies, wage records, safety programs, and handbooks pass every state inspection with flying colors.

NHDOL Joint Loss Management Committees

Under RSA 281-A:64, every NH employer with 15 or more employees must establish a documented Safety Committee with equal employee-employer representation and quarterly meetings.

RSA 275: Wage & Hour Protective Legislation

Strict notification mandates: employers must notify employees in writing of pay rate and paydays, and provide written notice prior to any wage reductions to avoid fines.

New Hampshire Youth Employment (RSA 276-A)

Navigating strict hours restrictions, mandatory youth employment certificates (working papers), and parental permission requirements for minors in hospitality and trades.

Worker Classification (RSA 281-A:2)

Ensuring independent contractors strictly meet New Hampshire's multi-factor statutory exemption criteria to prevent workers' comp stop-work orders and tax penalties.

NH Paid Family & Medical Leave (NH PFML)

Guidance on the Granite State's voluntary state PFML program, employee payroll deduction administration, and coordinating private wage replacement plans.

Personnel File Access (RSA 275:56)

Complying with mandatory employee access to personnel files, procedures for employee written rebuttals, and strict medical information confidentiality.
