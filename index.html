    HR outsourcing FAQs

    What is HR outsourcing?

    HR outsourcing means a dedicated partner handles your HR functions, such as compliance, compensation, recruiting and training, so you get expert support without building a full in-house department.

    Where do you work?

    HR2fit serves businesses in Massachusetts and New Hampshire, with offices in Walpole, MA and Tilton, NH. Services are delivered on site at your workplace.

    What does the free assessment include?

    An on-site look at your current HR practices, so we can point out gaps, risks and opportunities. Request one through the contact page and we'll arrange a time.

    Is HR2fit a fit for a small business?

    Yes. Our layered service model lets you start with only the services you need and expand as you grow, with affordability built in.

    Can you help with recruiting?

    Yes. Recruiting and staffing is one of our services, so you don't have to spend 40 to 60 hours running a search yourself.
